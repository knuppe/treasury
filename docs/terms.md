# Terms of use

[Português](terms.pt-BR.md)

Treasury's terms of use: the first-run screen, the full text and where the notices appear in the
screens. The legal source text is the pt-BR one, in [terms.pt-BR.md](terms.pt-BR.md); the app
uses the same copy in `locales/conteudo/pt-BR/termos.md` (a test checks that they are equal), with
the pt-PT, English and Spanish versions next to it (`locales/conteudo/<language>/termos.md`). The
full text at the end of this file is the English version the app shows (a test checks it too).

- **Core:** `crates/treasury-core/src/terms.rs`: the current version (`CURRENT`), the history and
  this installation's acceptance in `terms-accepted.json`, in the app folder and outside the vault
  (version and date/time). Nothing is sent. Commands `terms.status` and `terms.accept`, which work
  without a vault; accepting is only through the interface.
- **Interface:** `apps/desktop/src/features/terms/` (the "Before you start" gate, the card in
  Settings › About and support › Terms of use, the screen notices and the texts).

## When to ask for acceptance again

The terms have their own MAJOR.MINOR version, independent of the app.

- Fixing wording, a link or a translation without changing the meaning: 1.0 → 1.1. Nothing at
  startup; a discreet notice ("The terms of use were updated", with Read and Got it) and the
  history in Settings.
- A new feature that sends data out (cloud integration, sync, an updater that contacts a server):
  → 2.0, shows the screen and asks for acceptance.
- A change to the liability limits, the license or donations: → 2.0, asks for acceptance.
- A new risk area (PDF reports for an accountant, Open Finance, brokerage orders): → 2.0, asks
  for acceptance.
- A new country with its own tax rules: 1.x → 1.y, discreet notice, without blocking.

At startup, if the accepted MAJOR is lower than the current one, the screen appears with "What
changed" at the top (`HISTORY` in `terms.rs`). It applies per installation (system user); "View
as" does not ask for acceptance. The acceptance is not copied into each vault.

## First-run screen

It appears before creating or opening any vault (including the demo one), right after choosing
the language, in the chosen language (pt-BR, pt-PT, English or Spanish). No pre-checked box;
"Continue" stays disabled until the box is checked; "Quit" closes the app (the Tauri `quit_app`
command). Links to the full text and to the AGPL license (gnu.org, in the browser). The short
texts are in `locales/<language>/termos.ftl`.

## Where the notices appear

- **Taxes** and **IR** (Reports): footer "Estimate based on [source] from [date]. It does not
  replace the official tools of [the agency]": Receita Federal (Brazil), IRS (US), Autoridade
  Tributária e Aduaneira (Portugal) or "your country's tax authority".
- **Asset score, Portfolio, Retirement and Scenarios:** "Study tool. Not an investment
  recommendation."
- **Agent (panel):** "AI can make mistakes. Check before approving." always, and "This
  conversation will be sent to [provider]." in a new conversation with a cloud engine (API or
  CLI).
- **Ingestion e-mail (IMAP mailbox):** "Treasury will read the folders you choose using this app
  password. The password stays in the vault."
- **Vault creation:** the box "I understand that if I forget the password and do not have the
  recovery kit, I lose the data." (the app has the recovery kit, hence the mention).
