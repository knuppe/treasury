# Balance forecast and "how much can I spend today"

Two calculations made only with what the app already knows, without AI: the balance of each account
over the coming months and how much can be spent per day for the rest of the month.

## Balance forecast

The balance of each everyday account (checking, savings, cash, card, benefit, foreign; no brokers or
archived accounts) and the total in the base currency, day by day, from today up to 3, 6 or 12 months
(1 to 24 through the API). It starts from today's balance and adds what is already known to happen:

- **expected transactions**, those generated by recurrences and those entered by hand;
- **recurrences beyond the horizon already generated**, expanded by the rule up to the end of the forecast;
- **open card statements**, paid on the due date from the card's payment account, unless a payment is already expected for them;
- **installments of loans and financing** with a debit account, unless an expected transaction already exists in the contract's category within 7 days of the due date.

For each account the forecast brings the starting and ending balance, the minimum and its date, the steps (`points`, the balance after each day with movement) and the events with their origin. The `first_negative` field brings the first day the account goes negative. On a card it never appears, because a negative card balance is debt.

- Interface: **Accounts › Balance forecast**, with a chart per account and total, a "goes negative on DD/MM" warning and the table of what moves the balance. The chart only shows accounts in the base currency.
- Command: `forecast.balance` (`months`, `account_ids`). MCP tool: `get_balance_forecast`.
- Reminder: an account that goes negative in the next 30 days becomes an alert and a notification (see [notifications.md](notifications.md)).

## How much can I spend today

It is what is left of the month for everyday spending, divided by the days remaining (counting today):

```
disponível = receitas do mês (recebidas + previstas)
           − gastos até hoje (competência)
           − contas que ainda vencem no mês (previstos + parcelas sem previsto)
           − o que falta guardar para as metas (contribuição mensal − aportes do mês)
por dia    = disponível ÷ dias que faltam (zero quando não sobra)
hoje       = por dia − o que já foi gasto hoje
```

The `status` is `over` when nothing is left, `tight` when the amount per day is below 30% of what comes in per day, and `ok` otherwise. The `explanation` field brings the calculation written out.

- Interface: a highlight at the top of the **Dashboard** at the Beginner and Intermediate levels (only for the current month and the family view), with "How I calculated it".
- Command: `forecast.daily_allowance`. MCP tool: `get_daily_allowance`.

Code: `crates/treasury-core/src/forecast/`. Mock: `apps/desktop/src/api/mock/planning.ts`. The mock uses only expected transactions, without expanding recurrences and card statements.

## Annual expenses (estimate)

The forecast includes the essential annual expenses of the previous year (IPVA, IPTU, insurance),
twelve months later and for the same amount, with the origin `seasonal`. The rule, what avoids
double counting and the limits are in [learned-rules.md](learned-rules.md).
