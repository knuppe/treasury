# Brazil: reference data for the tax estimate (version 2026-11)

Data for the app's tax estimate (Conservative, Likely and High scenarios). Percentages in %, amounts in reais. Each number has a source (id in the sources table at the end and in `sources.md`). Compiled on 2026-10-17.

## How to read the scenarios

- Percentage of the price paid by the consumer that is tax on the product.
- The groups calculated from the IBGE National Accounts have the same value in all three scenarios (Conservative, Likely and High). The scenarios differ only where the source gives a range: fuels (Petrobras price composition), insurance and bank fees (rates from the law).
- The IBGE number counts only the taxes charged on the product (import tax, IPI, ICMS and other taxes minus subsidies). Taxes paid earlier in the chain and not recovered, payroll charges and corporate income tax are not included: the result is a floor.
- The values are national and from 2021, the last year of the Supply and Use Tables with 128 products.

**Monthly reduction 2026** (Lei 15.270/2025, art. 2 (inserts art. 3-A into Lei 9.250/1995); effective from 2026-01-01): Taxable income subject to monthly withholding up to R$ 5,000.00: a reduction of up to R$ 312.89 (so that the tax due is zero). From R$ 5,000.01 to R$ 7,350.00: reduction = R$ 978.62 - (0.133145 x taxable income subject to monthly withholding), decreasing linearly to zero at R$ 7,350.00. Above R$ 7,350.00: no reduction. The reduction is capped at the tax calculated by the progressive table (§ 1) and also applies to the 13th salary (§ 3).

Formula for the app: `income <= 5000: reduction = min(tax, 312.89); 5000 < income < 7350: reduction = min(tax, max(0, 978.62 - 0.133145*income)); income >= 7350: 0. 'income' = rendimentos tributáveis brutos do mês (antes das deduções); 'tax' = imposto da tabela progressiva sobre a base de cálculo após deduções.`

Check: With income of R$ 5.000 and the simplified discount (R$ 607.20): base 4,392.80 x 22.5% - 675.49 = 312.89, equal to the reduction cap.

### 13th salary (sources `lei_8134_1990`, `lei_7713_1988`, `lei_15270_2025`)

Exclusive taxation at source, separately from other income, on the full amount, in the month of payment; no withholding on advances; legal deductions allowed (dependents, social security); uses the monthly progressive table. From 2026 the reduction of art. 3-A (Lei 15.270) also applies to the 13th.

### High incomes from 2026 (source `lei_15270_2025`)

- 10% on profits and dividends paid by the same legal entity to the same individual above R$ 50,000.00 in the month, from 2026-01 (with exceptions for profits determined up to 2025).
