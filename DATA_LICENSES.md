# Licenses of the data bundled in Treasury

Treasury's code is AGPL-3.0-only (`LICENSE`). Some official data ships inside the app and
**keeps its own license**: it is not relicensed as AGPL. This file says what it is, where it came
from, what was transformed and under what terms. The same attribution text appears in the app
(Settings › About and support and the source line of the "Life timeline").

## UN: World Population Prospects 2024 (mortality tables)

- **Attribution:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population
  Division (2024). World Population Prospects 2024, Online Edition.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO (CC BY 3.0 IGO),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/
- **Origin:** https://population.un.org/wpp/downloads (CSV, complete life tables, medium
  variant): `WPP2024_Life_Table_Complete_Medium_{Both,Female,Male}_{1950-2023,2024-2100}.csv.gz`.
- **Where it is:** `crates/treasury-core/data/longevity/wpp2024.zip` (`index.json`, `qx.bin`,
  `e0_cohort.bin`), generated by `scripts/longevity-data.py`.
- **Data cut and transformed by Treasury:**
  - only the countries in the app's catalog (`crates/treasury-core/src/country/catalog.rs`), the
    three sexes (both, women, men), ages 0 to 100 and the medium variant;
  - `qx` only from 2024 to 2100, stored as 16-bit integers (qx × 65535, rounded);
  - the open group (100+) converted into the annual q of a constant force of mortality with the
    UN's life expectancy at that age (q = 1 − exp(−1/e100));
  - life expectancy at birth **by cohort** (born 1950 to 2026) calculated by Treasury from the
    1950 to 2100 rates, along the diagonal of each cohort (after 2100, repeating 2100); the UN
    does not publish this number;
  - in the app, the survival curve, the median, the "1 in 4", the "1 in 10" and the chance that at
    least one of the two adults is alive are Treasury calculations (docs/life-expectancy.md §4).
- **No endorsement:** the UN does not endorse Treasury, this app or this use of the data. The
  numbers are population averages, not predictions for one person.

## IBGE: Complete Mortality Tables 2024

- **Source:** IBGE (Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics), Tábuas Completas de
  Mortalidade 2024 (published on 2025-11-28),
  https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/sociais/populacao/9126-tabuas-completas-de-mortalidade.html
- **Where it is:** `ibge2024.json` inside the same zip (`ambos_os_sexos.xlsx`, `homens.xlsx` and
  `mulheres.xlsx`), only as a period reference for Brazil.
- **Transformation:** the probability of death went from "per thousand" to a fraction (0 to 1);
  only `qx` and `ex` remain, from 0 to 90 years (90 or more).
- **Terms:** IBGE allows reproduction citing the source; raw data and official acts have no
  copyright protection (Lei 9.610/98, art. 8, the Brazilian copyright law). The source appears in
  the app next to the number.

## IBGE: National Accounts System, Supply and Use Tables for 2021

- **Source:** IBGE, Sistema de Contas Nacionais, Tabelas de Recursos e Usos at the level of 68
  activities and 128 products (published on 2023-11-09),
  https://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Contas_Nacionais/Sistema_de_Contas_Nacionais/2021/tabelas_xls/tabelas_de_recursos_e_usos/
- **Where it is:** `docs/tax-data/research/br_ibge_tru_2021.json` (values of tables 1 and 2 for
  2021, unchanged) and, already calculated, the consumption percentages in
  `crates/treasury-core/src/tax_burden/data/br.json`.
- **Transformation:** each product's percentage = total taxes on the product, net of subsidies ÷
  total supply at consumer prices; a group with several products = average weighted by household
  consumption (`scripts/tax-data/br_consumo_ibge.py`). The percentages are Treasury calculations
  (docs/taxes.md).
- **Terms:** IBGE allows reproduction citing the source; raw data and official acts have no
  copyright protection (Lei 9.610/98, art. 8). The source appears in the app next to the number.

## National Treasury: spending by function (COFOG) and municipal revenue (Siconfi)

- **Source:** Tesouro Nacional (the Brazilian National Treasury) and Secretaria de Orçamento
  Federal, "Despesa por Função do Governo Geral: resultados de 2024" (2025-12-12),
  https://www.tesourotransparente.gov.br/publicacoes/cofog-despesas-por-funcao-do-governo-geral/2024/114
  and the Siconfi API, https://apidatalake.tesouro.gov.br/docs/siconfi/.
- **Where it is:** Table 1 of the report in `docs/tax-data/research/br_cofog_2024.json` and in
  `crates/treasury-core/src/tax_burden/data/br.json` (`spending`). The Siconfi data is not bundled:
  it is queried at the person's request and stays in their vault.
- **Transformation:** none in the table; the app splits the estimated tax by the percentages and
  divides the revenue by the report's population (docs/taxes.md).
- **Terms:** public data from a federal agency, published on the Tesouro Transparente portal; acts
  and official data have no copyright protection (Lei 9.610/98, art. 8). The license of each dataset
  on the portal was not checked. The source appears in the app next to the number.

## Update

At each new edition of the World Population Prospects (every two years) and at each IBGE table
(December): download the files by hand (`python3 -I scripts/longevity-data.py --urls`), generate
the zip again and check the numbers in docs/life-expectancy-validation.md.

## Time zones (IANA tz database)

- **Source:** tz database (tzdata), maintained by IANA, https://www.iana.org/time-zones.
- **License:** public domain (tzdata's LICENSE file).
- **Where it is:** `crates/treasury-core/src/market/zone.rs`: the names of the financial centers'
  time zones, each one's standard offset and the daylight saving rules in force, rewritten by hand
  in a table; no tzdata file goes inside the app.
- **Update:** when a country changes the rule, change the table and the test of the year's
  transition.

## Open Food Facts (name, nutrients, NOVA and allergens by barcode)

- **Attribution:** Open Food Facts and its contributors, https://world.openfoodfacts.org. In the
  app, every sheet with data from there says "Data: Open Food Facts (ODbL) · view the product", with
  the link to the product page.
- **License:** the database, Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0; each piece of data, Database Contents
  License (DbCL) 1.0; the photos, CC BY-SA 3.0 (the app does not download photos).
- **Use:** optional lookup (off by default in Settings › Data sources), only by barcode, once per
  product, stored in the family's vault. The vault is private and not distributed, so mixing it with
  purchases does not create a public derived database. If the installer ever carries an excerpt of
  the database, that file goes separately, under ODbL, with this notice.
- **No warranty and no medical use:** the data is indicative and may contain errors (Open Food
  Facts terms of use); the health screen says so. The Open Food Facts logo and the Nutri-Score seal
  (a Santé publique France mark) are not used; the Nutri-Score appears only as text.
- **Terms:** https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use

## CoinGecko (crypto asset data)

- **Use:** lookup through the public API without a key (market cap, supply, volume, categories and
  development data of the registered crypto assets), at a pace of 6.5 s between requests.
- **Attribution:** the public API asks for attribution with a link where the data appears
  (https://brand.coingecko.com/resources/attribution-guide). The app shows "Crypto asset data
  provided by CoinGecko" with the link in Settings › About and support, cites CoinGecko as the
  source on each piece of data in the crypto asset score and puts the link in the score's footer,
  next to the data. The data is not redistributed.
- **Terms:** https://www.coingecko.com/en/api_terms
