# Literature consulted

| Reference | Finding | Applicability to Brazil | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piotroski (2000), JAR | F-Score separates winners and losers among cheap stocks | Replications on B3 with mixed results | Adopted as a metric (earnings quality) and flag ≤ 2; not applied to banks |
| Altman (1968); Altman, Hartzell and Peck (1995) | Z and Z'' predict distress | Z'' designed for emerging markets | Adopted Z'' (metric + cap of 45 below 1.1) |
| Beneish (1999), FAJ | M-Score > −1.78 indicates manipulation | Variables available in the DFPs (Brazilian annual financial statements) | Adopted only as a red flag |
| Sloan (1996) | High accruals predict worse returns | Yes | Adopted |
| Novy-Marx (2013), JFE | Gross profitability predicts returns | To be tested with NEFIN | Adopted |
| Greenblatt (2005) | Magic Formula (EY + ROC) | Yes | EY in valuation; ROC as a diagnostic |
| Fama & French (2015); Asness, Frazzini & Pedersen (2019) | Profitability, conservative investment and quality | NEFIN-USP factors to be checked | Structure of the dimensions |
| Cooper, Gulen & Schill (2008) | High asset growth predicts lower returns | To be tested | Band metric |
| Pontiff & Woodgate (2008) | Net issuance predicts lower returns | Yes | Dilution + flag > 5% |
| Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) | 12-1 momentum | Yes | Low weight |
| Frazzini & Pedersen (2014) | Low volatility | Yes | Stability |
| Basel III; Bacen (IF.data) | Minimum capital of 10.5% with conservation buffer | Brazilian rule | Anchor and flag (< 11.5% → cap of 40) |
| CAMELS | Bank supervision framework | Yes | Bank dimensions |
| Nareit (FFO/AFFO) | Definitions for REITs | FIIs (Brazilian real estate funds) report financial result, not FFO | Pending (use recurring result) |
